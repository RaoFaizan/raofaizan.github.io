Ph.D. Candidate in Artificial Intelligence · Kyung Hee University, South Korea

I build language and vision models that have to work under real constraints — limited compute, clinical accuracy requirements, and outputs that need to be verifiable. My doctoral research covers multi-agent LLM systems for cardiovascular diagnostics, model compression for healthcare deployment, and vision–language modeling on clinical imaging.

Research areas

Where my work sits, and the through-line connecting it: making capable models small enough, grounded enough, and trustworthy enough to deploy.

Multi-agent LLM systems

Orchestrating specialised agents for clinical diagnostics — task decomposition, tool use, and coordination between reasoning and retrieval components.

Efficient language models

Quantization, knowledge distillation and structured pruning to cut inference cost while holding diagnostic accuracy.

Vision–language modeling

Clinical question answering and automated report generation over chest X-ray corpora such as MIMIC.

Neuro-symbolic verification

Proof-reasoner components that check LLM-generated claims against a symbolic knowledge base to reduce unsupported output.

Visual surveillance

Person and vehicle re-identification across non-overlapping cameras; attention mechanisms for discriminative feature learning.

Intelligent energy systems

LSTM-driven demand forecasting and battery-storage scheduling for automated demand response.
