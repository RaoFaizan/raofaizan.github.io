Automating medical multi agent workflows from natural language using LLMs accepted C. Bold, R. Faizan, S. Lee, S. T. Kim Korea Computer Congress 2026, Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers, Jeju, June 2026. Not yet online.
Hybrid retrieval augmented generation for chest pain differential diagnosis: recall gains and calibration costs accepted C. Bold, R. Faizan, C. H. Hua, S. M. Kang, S. Lee, S. T. Kim Asia Pacific Advanced Network Conference, IEEE, 2026. Not yet online. We benchmarked three retrieval variants on 165 case vignettes from PubMed Central. Hybrid retrieval recovers more of the right diagnoses in distribution, but it also becomes overconfident about familiar ones when the case is unusual.

Conference abstracts

Longitudinal trajectories of guideline directed medical therapy and 12 month mortality in acute heart failure: insights from the KorHF-III registry presented D. J. Choi, R. Faizan, C. Bold, S. Y. Lee, J. H. Kim, S. Jung, S. M. Kang, B. S. Yoo, H. J. Cho, J. C. Youn, J. J. Park, M. Yoon European Society of Cardiology Congress 2026, Munich, 28 to 31 August 2026, abstract 87857. We built a knowledge graph from 7,351 acute heart failure patients across 47 Korean centres and used it to follow what happens to people whose guideline directed therapy is reduced after discharge. Mortality is higher in that group, and it stays higher after adjusting for baseline severity. Work with cardiology departments at five Korean university hospitals.
