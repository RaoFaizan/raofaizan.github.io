Conference abstracts

Longitudinal Trajectories of Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy and 12-Month Mortality in Acute Heart Failure: Insights from the KorHF-III Registry Accepted D. J. Choi, R. Faizan, C. Bold, S. Y. Lee, J. H. Kim, S. Jung, S. M. Kang, B. S. Yoo, H. J. Cho, J. C. Youn, J. J. Park, M. Yoon European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Congress 2026, Munich, August 2026 (abstract no. 87857). A knowledge graph built from 7,351 acute heart-failure patients across 47 Korean centres, used to show that de-escalating guideline-directed medical therapy is independently associated with higher 12-month mortality. Work with cardiology departments at five Korean university hospitals.
