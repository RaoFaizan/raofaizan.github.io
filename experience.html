Doctoral Researcher, Agentic AI & Medical Knowledge Systems

Mar 2024 – Present

Kyung Hee University · Gyeonggi-do, South Korea

  • Lead the multi-agent workstream of Explainable Logical Reasoning for Medical Knowledge Generation (IITP-2022-0-00078, Korean Ministry of Science and ICT): specialised agents for cardiovascular diagnosis, risk explanation and patient health literacy.
  • Construct medical knowledge graphs directly from raw clinical registry data. The KorHF-III heart-failure registry (7,351 patients, 47 centres) became a 7,439-node, 114,505-edge graph, queried through a HybridRAG pipeline that combines graph traversal with dense and sparse retrieval.
  • Design the context-management, safety and alignment layer: inter-agent context passing, scope limits on clinical claims, and a proof reasoner that verifies generated statements against a symbolic knowledge base before they reach a user.
  • Shrink clinical models for hospital-side inference through quantization, knowledge distillation and structured pruning, including BANQ, a budget-aware nested quantization scheme serving one low-bit checkpoint across several memory budgets.
  • Build vision–language models over the MIMIC chest X-ray corpus for clinical question answering and automated report generation; co-author grant proposals and deliverables.
