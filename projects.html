Lightweight Language Model for Healthcare

2024 – Present

Kyung Hee University · Korean government-funded

  • Problem. Clinical language models capable enough to support diagnosis are too large to deploy affordably, and their outputs cannot be taken on trust in a medical setting.
  • Approach. A neuro-symbolic small language model covering health literacy, disease detection, treatment recommendation and medical report generation — paired with a proof reasoner that verifies generated claims against a symbolic knowledge base.
  • My role. Owner of the model-efficiency workstream (quantization, knowledge distillation, structured pruning) and the vision–language pipeline over the MIMIC chest X-ray corpus.

AI-Based Energy Management Solution — Japan Market

2024 – Present

Kyung Hee University · Industry-funded

  • Problem. Grid operators need household and facility demand to flex in response to supply conditions and price signals, without users having to think about it.
  • Approach. A platform spanning grid automation, automated demand response, distributed energy resource integration, balance group formation and flexible pricing.
  • My role. Built the Self Demand Response (SDR) component — dataset unification across heterogeneous consumption sources, LSTM forecasting models driving the SDR policy, and the battery-storage scheduling algorithm.

IAB-Net — Informative & Attention-Based Person Re-Identification

2020 – 2021

M.S. thesis · Machine Vision & Intelligent Systems Lab, NUST

  • Problem. Matching the same person across non-overlapping surveillance cameras breaks down under viewpoint, illumination and background variation.
  • Approach. An attention module that suppresses background clutter and emphasises discriminative body regions, improving matching robustness across cameras.
  • Outcome. Published and presented at ICoDT2 2021 (IEEE). Owned the full cycle: dataset curation, model design, ablation studies and write-up.
