Explainable Logical Reasoning for Medical Knowledge Generation

2024 to present

Kyung Hee University. Funded by IITP, Korean Ministry of Science and ICT, project IITP-2022-0-00078.

A language model capable enough to support a cardiac diagnosis is too large to deploy affordably, and its output cannot be taken on trust anywhere near a patient. This project is an attempt at both halves of that.

The system is a set of agents, each with a narrow job, whose reasoning is anchored in knowledge graphs built from real clinical data rather than from scraped text. The KorHF-III heart failure registry covers 7,351 patients across 47 centres; as a graph that is 7,439 nodes and 114,505 edges. A second graph comes from a chest pain knowledge base curated by clinicians. Queries run through a hybrid retrieval pipeline over a graph database, reachable as a tool through MCP, and a proof reasoner checks generated claims against a symbolic knowledge base before they surface.

I lead the agent workstream. That covers building the graphs, the layer that manages context and keeps claims inside their evidence, the compression work that gets models onto hospital hardware, and the vision and language pipeline over MIMIC-CXR.

The registry analysis was presented at the European Society of Cardiology Congress 2026 in Munich, with cardiology departments from five Korean university hospitals.

Hybrid AI Multi Agent System for Data Driven Energy Operations

2024 to present

Kyung Hee University. MSIT ITRC programme, DESIRe Center (Data-driven Energy System Innovation Research Center).

Energy operators hold grid data, building load data and market data in formats that do not talk to each other, and they need demand to flex against supply and price without anyone having to think about it.

I built the hybrid retrieval system that sits over NREL open energy datasets, so an operational question can be answered across those incompatible sources. Before that I built the self demand response component: unifying consumption data from several sources, training the LSTM forecasting models that drive the response policy, and writing the battery storage scheduling algorithm.

IAB-Net, person re-identification

2020 to 2021

Master's thesis. Machine Vision and Intelligent Systems Lab, NUST.

Matching the same person across cameras that do not overlap falls apart under changes in viewpoint, lighting and background. I built an attention module that suppresses background clutter and weights the parts of the body that actually distinguish people, which made matching hold up better across cameras.

I did the whole thing end to end: curating the dataset, designing the model, running the ablations, writing it up and presenting it at ICoDT2 2021. The code is on GitHub.
